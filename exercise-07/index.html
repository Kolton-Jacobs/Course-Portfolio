My Favorite Video Games

This is my curated collection of favorite video games. Each game here represents something meaningful to me, whether it's a beloved classic, an innovative indie title, or a recent release that captured my attention. This collection showcases how responsive design allows me to adapt my content across all screen sizes.

XBOX

Video Games | XBOX

The XBOX is a gaming console that was initially not my first choice. I grew up as a playstation kid and upon the release of HALO Reach I made a change and never looked back. There are some days where I dont even have the urge to play, but I do find myself booting it up every once in a while and getting lost in another world.

Halo

Video Games | XBOX

This is the one that started it all for me, the reason I fell in love with gaming. Of course I did not start with the original Halo, but I did start with Halo Reach. I was instantly hooked and I have been a fan of the franchise ever since. I will say they haven't been able to capture the magic of the original trilogy, but I still enjoy playing the newer titles. The multiplayer is always a blast and I have made some great memories with friends over the years.

Fallout

Video Games | XBOX

Fallout is another one that got me hooked on gaming. I remember wathcing my cousin play it long ago and just being amazed at all you could do. The open-world exploration and the post-apocalyptic setting really drew me in and keeps drawing me in to this day. I have spent countless hours exploring the wasteland and completing quests, and I always find something new to discover.

GTA

Video Games | XBOX

GTA has always held a special place in my heart. I would say it would predate the xbox time period for me. I remember waking up at night and seeing dad driving around in San Andreas and just being amazed at the level of detail and freedom you had in the game. I have spent countless hours playing GTA and I still find myself coming back to it from time to time.

Forza Horizon

Video Games | XBOX

Forza Horizon is a more recent addition to my collection, but it has quickly become one of my favorites. The open-world racing and the beautiful graphics make it a joy to play. I love exploring the different locations and trying out all the different cars. It's a great way to unwind and have some fun. I also love the music in the game, it really adds to the atmosphere and makes the experience even more enjoyable. I highly recommend it to anyone who loves racing games or just wants to have a good time.

Baldurs Gate 3

Video Games | XBOX

Baldurs Gate 3 has been a much more recent addition to my catalogue of favorite games. I have always been a fan of the Dungeons and Dragons universe, and this game has really captured that essence. The story is engaging, the characters are well-developed, and the gameplay is both challenging and rewarding. I have spent countless hours exploring the world, completing quests, and making choices that affect the entire story of the game.
