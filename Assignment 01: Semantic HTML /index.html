My Top 5 Video Games of 2008

These are five of my favorite video games that were released in the iconic gaming year of 2008.

  1. Fallout 3

    Fallout 3 is an open-world role-playing game, that is set in a post-apocalyptic version of Washington, D.C. I enjoy the freedom that the game gives the player to explore, make choices, and complete missions in different ways and that affect the outcome of the game.

  2. Call of Duty: World at War

    Call of Duty: World at War is a World War II first-person shooter. It includes an intense campaign, multiplayer, and introduced the popular Zombies game mode to the Call of Duty series.

  3. Grand Theft Auto IV

    Grand Theft Auto IV is an open-world action game set in Liberty City. The game stands out because of its large open world, story, characters, and the amount of freedom that is given to the player.

  4. Guitar Hero World Tour

    Guitar Hero World Tour is a music rhythm game that expanded the series by allowing players to use guitar, bass, drums, and vocals. It was a great multiplayer game because several people could play together locally.

  5. Left 4 Dead

    Left 4 Dead is a cooperative zombie survival game where four players work together to survive large groups of infected enemies. Its teamwork and replayability make it one of my favorite games that was released in 2008.
